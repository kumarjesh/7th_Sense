📱 4. On-Screen HUD Quick Actions (For Sighted Testers & Low-Vision Users)

🧠 Describe Scene Button

Snaps active camera frame and invokes Google Gemini 2.0 Flash to synthesize a detailed spatial orientation paragraph with clock directions.

📖 Read Text (OCR) Button

Activates automatic sign and text reading. Point phone at signs, food packages, or mail. Deduplicates repeated text automatically.

💡 Light Tone Button

Starts continuous audio pitch oscillator. High frequency whistle indicates facing a bright light source or daylight window.

🎯 Seek Target Button

Cycles search targets (door, chair, stairs, etc.). Emits 3D binaural directional beacon sound that centers when facing target.

💵 Cash / Currency Button

Snaps frame and identifies banknote currency and denomination (e.g. "Twenty US Dollars") to prevent payment errors.

⚙️ Settings & Torch Buttons

Top HUD bar gives direct access to flashlight toggle, dark/light theme switch, pause/resume, and settings configuration dialog.

🔊 5. 3D Spatial Audio Radar & Proximity Band Guide

Proximity Band Estimated Distance Spatial Sonar Audio Cue Voice Speech Behavior
CLEAR / SAFE Beyond 3.5 meters Low-frequency intermittent pulse (1.2s cadence) Ambient path status spoken every 15 seconds.
CAUTION 1.5 to 3.5 meters Medium-pitch pulse (500ms cadence), pans stereo left/right Speaks obstacle class and zone: "Chair right caution".
DANGER / CRITICAL Under 1.2 meters (cane reach) High-pitch rapid beacon (150ms cadence) + haptic buzz Immediate audio ducking & interrupt: "Stop! Person center danger".

🔧 6. Zero-Typing Project Configuration via .env

🔑 Instant Google Gemini AI Configuration

You never need to type or paste an API key on your mobile phone screen. Configure it directly in the project file:

  • Open .env in the project root on your computer.
  • Set: VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key_here
  • Get a free key (no credit card required) at aistudio.google.com.
  • When deploying to Vercel, add VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY under Project Settings ➔ Environment Variables. Every mobile phone visiting your Vercel URL will automatically load the key!

💡 7. Testing Checklist for New Users

1️⃣ Test Edge Collision

Walk toward a chair or wall. Hear the sonar tempo speed up and pan left/right. At 1.2m, verify the critical interrupt: "Stop! [Object] danger".

2️⃣ Test Gemini Scene Narrator

Double-tap with two fingers in any room. Listen to Gemini describe furniture, doors, and clear paths using clock directions.

3️⃣ Test Text Reader

Swipe up with two fingers. Aim camera at a book, food box, or street sign. Hear the speech synthesizer read the words.

4️⃣ Test Light Detector

Swipe down with two fingers. Aim camera toward a window or lamp. Notice the oscillator whistle rise into a high pitch.
